# Art for Tower Defense

Pictures made with an image model (ComfyUI on our own computer) to make the
game look more polished. Nothing here is a photo of anyone.

- `layout/` - the meadow as the game draws it now: the trail, the stone pads
  and the trees, with no castle, monsters or knights. Give these to the image
  model as the starting picture (image-to-image) so a painted background
  lines up exactly with where the trail and pads are in the game.
  `layout-1344x768.png` is a size SDXL and FLUX like; `layout-1920x1080.png`
  is the game's own size.
