# The Orlis look — work in progress

A shared style for every Orlis Play game, like Roblox or Fortnite: the same
on-screen pieces and building blocks everywhere, while each game keeps its own
world and mood. **Not final.** It gets settled once Last Known Position and Pop
Party are release-ready and a third game shows what really needs sharing.

See `orlis-look.png` for the first mockup.

## Decided so far

- **Two views, both supported.** First-person (Pop Party: your hands are
  glowing rings in your player colour) and third-person (Last Known Position:
  your character, with a marker in your colour). A game can switch between them.
- **World pieces come from Last Known Position.** Its trees, crystals, sky,
  creatures and lighting are the reference art — not the simpler shapes in the
  first mockup.
- **Player colours** stay as they are: blue, orange, green, purple.
- **No custom characters yet.** Held for later.

## Ideas to try, not decided

- One set of on-screen pieces: rounded "glass" pills for score, timer, gems and
  lives; short messages ("Get ready!", "Nice!", "Step back in"); a message card
  any game can use (Nova in LKP, a host in Pop Party).
- One set of move cards (Jump, Duck, Lean left/right, Reach up, Hand to ear,
  Wave, Pause), drawn and worded the same everywhere.
- Fredoka for numbers and short words; Inter for sentences.
- Shared game sounds for gems, scoring, the countdown.

## Open questions

- **The HUD with four players**, in each view. First-person: four score pills
  where hands don't reach (two each side of the timer?). Third-person: a marker
  over each of the four crew, plus a small player tray (name, colour, health)?
  To mock up when we pick the look back up.
- How much of Last Known Position's helmet/sci-fi interface stays its own, and
  how much becomes shared.
- Whether Pop Party's world moves to Last Known Position's pieces.
