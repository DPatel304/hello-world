1 · Toy figures

Toy figures

Last Known Position's look: chunky shapes, bold outlines, lit from the left. Reads clearly from the sofa and suits action and adventure.

Good for: adventure and action, reading from the sofa; Claude can draw it easily.
Watch out: busy when lots is on screen.

2 · Soft clay

Soft clay

No outlines; soft, round and glossy, like vinyl toys or clay. Gentle and cosy, very "little kids" - feels like a 3D game without being one.

Good for: the youngest players, calm games; feels premium.
Watch out: softer to read at a distance; gradients cost a little more to draw.

3 · Sticker book

Sticker book

Flat, bright colours with a white sticker edge, on cheerful patterned backgrounds. Pop Party's energy; very readable on any background.

Good for: party games, any background, kids spotting themselves fast.
Watch out: the white edge needs an extra pass each frame.

4 · Storybook paper

Storybook paper

Cut-paper shapes in warm, softer colours with little paper shadows. Calm and crafted - lovely for gentle games and stories.

Good for: gentle stories, nature, calmer evenings.
Watch out: softer colours read less boldly on a TV.

5 · Night glow

Night glow

Dark worlds with glowing outlines in each child's colour. Exciting and magical - great for space and night games, less so for sunny ones.

Good for: space, night and music games; very striking.
Watch out: only works on dark worlds; glow is the costliest to draw.

6 · Crayon

Crayon

Looks drawn by a child: waxy colours, wobbly pencil outlines, on drawing paper. Warm and personal - their avatar looks like their own drawing.

Good for: creative games, drawing and colouring games, Create.
Watch out: textures are harder for Claude to copy in creators' games.

7 · Watercolour

Watercolour

Soft painted edges and gentle washes of colour. Dreamy and calm, like a picture book.

Good for: wind-down games, nature, music.
Watch out: low contrast on a bright TV; blurring costs more to draw.

8 · Pixel art

Pixel art

Chunky retro pixels, like classic video games. Grown-ups love it; kids read it as "a real video game".

Good for: arcade games, retro challenges, a special event.
Watch out: small faces lose detail; less "made for little kids".

9 · Comic

Comic

Thick ink, flat bright colours and halftone dots, with bursts behind the action. Loud and energetic.

Good for: superhero-ish action, big moments, results screens.
Watch out: loud for the youngest; can feel busy.

10 · Felt

Felt and stitches

Soft fabric with fuzzy texture and stitched edges, like a handmade puppet show. Tactile and cosy.

Good for: the youngest, pretend play, animals and farms.
Watch out: texture is subtle on a TV; harder for Claude to copy.
